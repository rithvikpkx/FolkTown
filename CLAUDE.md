# FolkTown

## What FolkTown is

FolkTown is a Purdue-themed first-person shooter that runs in a browser tab. The West Lafayette campus is the map, and the first goal is that walking around it feels like the real campus. Gunplay, multiplayer for up to 16 players, single-player with bots, vehicles and a living campus are built on top of that, in stages.

Assets come first, code second, and **any asset may be used** (owner decision, 12 November 2026): download models, textures, photographs, sounds and fonts from anywhere freely downloadable, whatever the licence; the owner handles licensing and credits with the authors after the fact. Rules that remain: record every asset's source URL, author and licence status in `docs/assets.md` (mark unknown or restrictive ones `needs-clearance`) so the owner can follow up; do not bypass paywalls, logins, DRM or anti-scraping, and do not rip assets out of commercial games or purchased products; generate in code only what no asset can be found for. Use as many real photographs of each building as can be found, for reference and for textures. Paid AI 3D generation is banked for later, if quality still falls short.
